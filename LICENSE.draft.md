# Company Installer License Framework (Draft)

This document outlines terms for company review only. It does not grant new rights to use, modify, or redistribute any content, and it does not replace any existing valid license. Once the terms are confirmed, they should be incorporated into a company-approved `LICENSE` or software license document.

## Scope

This draft covers only installer code and documentation for which the company holds the relevant rights. Third-party drivers, KOs, DTBOs, ISP/NITO files, and their source content remain subject to their respective applicable licenses. See the [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Items for Confirmation

| Item | Details for company review |
|---|---|
| Legal rights holder | Official English company name, applicable years, and ownership |
| License recipients | Applicability to internal teams, FAEs, partners, and customers |
| Permitted use | Conditions covering products, purposes, devices, and regions |
| Copying and modification | Whether company-owned code may be copied or modified, and under what conditions |
| Redistribution | Whether archives may be shared, distributed through public websites, or repackaged |
| Retention of notices | Copyright, license, and third-party notices that must be retained |
| Support and warranty | Technical support, warranty, liability scope, and applicable limitations |
| Other terms | Termination, governing law, and existing company software licensing policies |

If the company already has an applicable standard software license, first confirm whether it can be reused to avoid introducing conflicting terms.
